Bottom lineThe debtor's reports carry prospective, post-entitlement values (Bella Mar $20M, Green Phair $50M, Fund III $5M land) as current value. The Examiner could not value Bella Mar or Green Phair and cut Fund III to $800K. The as-is value of each interest has to be built from scenarios, debt, waterfalls and DLOC/DLOM.

Headline findings

  • Fund III's parcels are the 18.7-ac off-site MHPA habitat land for Green Phair's Renzulli Estates (DEIR Aug 2025), to be dedicated to the City with an endowment; not buildable R-1 land.
  • Green Phair is not approved and does not own the land: Draft EIR only (8/19/2025); Renzulli Properties LLC is owner of record.
  • Bella Mar is entitled for 380 apartments, not Lennar's 166 townhomes; the $19.5M LOI needs Lennar's own re-entitlement, so it is a contingent, prospective price.
  • Phair's Bella Mar waterfall reproduces to the dollar: $11,686,250 gross, $8,776,045 net at $19.5M; accrued interest implies about 16.8% simple a year.
  • Trustee “economic rights only” needs counsel's opinion: §17705.02 vs. §541(c)(1) and In re Albright; Fund III (100%) may carry no DLOC.
