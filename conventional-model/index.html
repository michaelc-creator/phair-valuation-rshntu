Construction loan, schedule and sales absorption

Equity funds land and costs first, up to (1 − LTC) × budget. Then the loan draws up to its commitment. Interest accrues monthly on the drawn balance and is capitalized when the interest reserve is on. Each closing repays release % × the loan allocated to that unit (by revenue share), and the last closing sweeps the balance. Sale cash still needed for scheduled construction costs is held back first, and anything left goes to equity. Costs above the loan budget, like carry and the loan fee, are funded by equity.

Methodology and USPAP note (draft)

  • Method. This is a subdivision-development (residual) model for a hypothetical conventional for-sale build-out. Gross sales come from the finished-unit sale-price anchors. Deduct selling costs, the developer's profit target, vertical hard and soft costs, contingency, defect liability, site work and impact fees to get the pre-finance residual, which uses the same method as the Property 1 v1 workbook. Then deduct construction interest, the loan fee and property-tax carry from the monthly engine, and divide by (1 + land closing %), to get the residual land value. That value is undiscounted. It applies no time, entitlement-risk or distress discount and no DLOC/DLOM.
  • Hypothetical conditions. The entitled program is 380 rental apartments (280 market + 100 affordable) [6][7]. A for-sale conventional build of 380 units is a hypothetical condition, and so is any mix the user enters. No 300-unit plan exists in the record. The 166-unit preset follows the national homebuilder's LOI program, which requires the buyer to re-entitle [1].
  • Extraordinary assumptions. Site work uses the 166-unit study [11]. Fees use a single-family schedule. Condo $/sf is proxied from new attached sales in the market-area ZIP [14]. Affordable for-sale value is 50% of market $/sf, adapted from v1 A-4. The sales pace is a proxy taken from apartment lease-up research [9].
  • USPAP. Standards 1–2 (2024 USPAP) require the appraiser to support every input, reconcile with the sales-comparison and LOI evidence, and disclose all hypothetical conditions and extraordinary assumptions. This page is a sensitivity tool for that work. It is not a USPAP-compliant appraisal report [P].
  • Not deducted. Secured debt and liens are excluded unless entered in the cap table: the secured lender's $2,272,710, the NOD lien $31,606, and the development partner's claim $2,559,917 (case document). Also not deducted: the development-partner JV waterfall (case document), and Prop 13 reassessment or HOA formation costs.
