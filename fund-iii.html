  • Jeffrey D. Phair, 100%; and Phair Real Estate Acquisition Fund III LLC, 100% — $800,000. The examiner assigned an $800,000 current value based on the late-2024 purchase price (Doc 362, Pgs. 12–13, 15). The same purchase price is used as an input in the development-cost model on this page.
  • Caveats, not used to change the box figure: the examiner noted a $2,000,000 loan and a $1,050,000 note receivable “which may affect value,” with no supporting detail (Doc 362, Pg. 15). If the loan is valid and secured on the land, equity could be reduced, including to zero. The model’s mid “Value to Sponsor (after DLOC; $2M loan per toggle)” is $241,147. The debtor has also booked a $4,000,000 figure. Those alternatives are not shown in the boxes.

Open questions

  1. Land basis / acreage: Assessor 17.09 ac vs. DEIR 18.7 ac; acreage of -09 (derived 1.19 ac) and its use code. (Every $/ac indication.; ask: Title / survey)
  2. Liens: Documents for the $2M loan and the $1.05M note receivable; is the loan secured on the land? (A valid lien can take equity to $0.; ask: Debtor / trustee)
  3. Mitigation terms: Is there an agreement with Landowner/Property 2 to sell or credit the land as mitigation? Price? (Scenario A value and weight.; ask: Counsel / applicant)
  4. Dedication: Would the City require fee dedication without payment, and who funds the endowment (current PAR quote)? (Scenario C; endowment in 1996 $ is understated.; ask: City regional habitat plan)
  5. Scenario weights: Equal 1/3 weights are judgment; tie to Property 2 approval probability. (Weighted value.; ask: Analyst / counsel)
  6. Holding costs: Any HOA, brush/fire clearance, liability insurance or management cost on the preserve. (Small carry items not in file.; ask: Debtor)
  7. Ownership: Record owner is "Sponsor Real Estate Acquisitions Fund LLC" per profiles vs. Property 3; confirm vesting. (Interest valued.; ask: Title)
  8. Discounts: DLOM not applied; DLOC 0-5% per notes. (Value to Sponsor.; ask: Business valuator)

1. Ownership, control & legal rights3 steps · 3.0 hrs
DoneStepHrsDefense attack vector
Entity identity & title vesting: deed vests title in “Phair Real Estate Acquisitions Fund LLC,” not “...Acquisition Fund III LLC” 1.0 If title isn’t in Fund III, Fund III may own nothing. The first question opposing counsel will ask.
Trustee control of a single-member LLC: In re Albright, 291 B.R. 538 / 11 U.S.C. §541(c)(1) vs. Cal. Corp. Code §17705.02 1.0 If the trustee steps into full control, DLOC is about 0. A minority discount here would be attacked.
Find any agreement committing the parcels to Green Phair / Renzulli Estates mitigation 1.0 If the land is already committed for dedication, its exchange value may be close to zero.
