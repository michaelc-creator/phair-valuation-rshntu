1. Ownership, control & legal rights3 steps · 3.0 hrs
DoneStepHrsDefense attack vector
Entity identity & title vesting: deed vests title in “Phair Real Estate Acquisitions Fund LLC,” not “...Acquisition Fund III LLC” 1.0 If title isn’t in Fund III, Fund III may own nothing. The first question opposing counsel will ask.
Trustee control of a single-member LLC: In re Albright, 291 B.R. 538 / 11 U.S.C. §541(c)(1) vs. Cal. Corp. Code §17705.02 1.0 If the trustee steps into full control, DLOC is about 0. A minority discount here would be attacked.
Find any agreement committing the parcels to Green Phair / Renzulli Estates mitigation 1.0 If the land is already committed for dedication, its exchange value may be close to zero.
