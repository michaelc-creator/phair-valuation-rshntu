1. Scope, valuation date & independence (FRE 702)3 steps · 1.5 hrs · $188
DoneStepEvidence requiredHrsDefense attack vector
Matter-wide engagement letter, retention & FRE 702 independence (1/3 of a shared 1.5-hr item; counted once across the 3 LLCs) Signed engagement letter naming the client (Pennington Law for creditor Renzulli Properties LLC vs. the estate/trustee), scope, $125/hr non-contingent fee; conflicts check; written disclosure of all prior contact with Phair, Renzulli, Red Tail and Lennar. 0.5 Opposing counsel's first question: “who is paying you and for what result?” A vague retention or contingent fee frames the work as advocacy, not expertise.
Valuation date and premise: standalone open-space value vs. value as Renzulli Estates mitigation Counsel’s instruction on the premise; date of any trustee control; DEIR dependency on Green Phair approvals. 0.5 Mixing a contingent mitigation value with an as-is standard is an easy reliability attack.
Reliance log and data request (no Fund III periodic reports in the file) Request Fund III periodic reports, GL, bank statements and returns. Debtor says no business before 2024/2025, yet a 2023 CA return was filed. 0.5 Thin records support a higher DLOM; if missing, the defense says the facts were insufficient.
2. Ownership, control & legal rights3 steps · 3.0 hrs · $375
DoneStepEvidence requiredHrsDefense attack vector
Entity identity & title vesting: deed vests title in “Phair Real Estate Acquisitions Fund LLC,” not “...Acquisition Fund III LLC” Recorded grant deed Doc # 2024-0286859; SOS records for both names; Fund III operating agreement; corrective deed or counsel’s opinion if a scrivener’s error. 1.0 If title isn’t in Fund III, Fund III may own nothing. The first question opposing counsel will ask.
Trustee control of a single-member LLC: In re Albright, 291 B.R. 538 / 11 U.S.C. §541(c)(1) vs. Cal. Corp. Code §17705.02 Counsel’s written legal opinion; the operating agreement (if any); any conversion/trustee order. 1.0 If the trustee steps into full control, DLOC is about 0. A minority discount here would be attacked.
Find any agreement committing the parcels to Green Phair / Renzulli Estates mitigation Option, purchase, dedication or mitigation agreements among Fund III, Green Phair, Renzulli or the City; MHPA boundary adjustment application documents. 1.0 If the land is already committed for dedication, its exchange value may be close to zero.
3. Underlying asset value & cash flows3 steps · 3.5 hrs · $438
DoneStepEvidence requiredHrsDefense attack vector
Title, parcel data and acreage reconciliation Preliminary title report for APNs 359-210-09/-10/-11; assessor (17.09 ac); DEIR (18.7 ac); survey; easements and conservation restrictions. 1.0 A 1.6-acre gap affects every $/acre calculation.
Zoning, MHPA and use restrictions City zoning and MHPA maps; DEIR §3.3.2.2 (off-site MHPA addition, fee-title dedication, perpetual endowment); assessor use codes (-09/-10 open space; -11 R-1); recorded covenants. 1.0 Valuing the 11.8-ac parcel as buildable R-1 when slated for habitat dedication is a major overstatement.
Highest & best use and scenarios: (A) mitigation value to the Renzulli Estates builder × p(project); (B) standalone open-space sale; (C) dedication for no consideration, $0 or negative after endowment Inputs from the Green Phair model; endowment cost estimate; any buyer interest; City acquisition program rates. 1.5 Fund III depends on Green Phair. Ignoring that, or counting it on both pages, would be attacked.
